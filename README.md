The main problem I encountered during my project was my attempt to have an ever moving level with an infinite number of platforms. However, over time I realized that was impractical and shifted to my current design where the platforms shift based on player interaction with another object. My main secondary problems were centered around player interaction with other objects, updating player location, and platform generation. Both platform generation and player interaction with said platforms was the first item I fixed through researching array lists and limiting their generative abilities. Updating player movement was something I struggled with for an embarrassingly long time and I finally realized was not working because I had code placed in the wrong area, which was simultaneously a relief and a curse. A lot of player movement and platform interaction was informed by a series of videos by Chris Whitmire Lessons on YouTube.
